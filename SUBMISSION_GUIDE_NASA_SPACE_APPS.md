## 2. High-Level Project Summary (Copy & Paste)
AeroData is an interactive Earth science visualization and data sonification platform built for **The Earth Information Jukebox** challenge. It converts multi-decade NASA Earth Information Center (EIC) climate telemetry—covering atmospheric CO₂, methane, global temperature anomalies, sea level rise, and Arctic sea ice extent—into harmonic audio tracks and interactive geospatial dashboards, making complex planetary data accessible through both sight and sound.

## 3. How It Works & NASA Data Integration
- **Data Engine:** Processes multi-decade atmospheric and cryospheric time-series trends alongside global baseline observatory coordinates (Mauna Loa, Ny-Ålesund, ATTO, Barrow, Cape Grim, South Pole, Izaña).
- **Acoustic Jukebox:** Normalizes environmental indicators and synthesizes multi-harmonic `.WAV` compositions across selectable musical scales so users can listen to planetary shifts over time.
